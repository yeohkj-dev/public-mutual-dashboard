How performance is calculated: price return from NAV (net asset value per unit) published by Public Mutual, between the latest price date and the closest earlier pricing day. 3Y and 5Y are annualised. Distributions (dividends) are not added back, so income-paying funds look lower than their total return — check the fund's distribution policy in the detail panel. "Region" and "theme" are inferred from the fund name plus Public Mutual's mandate tag (Local / Foreign / Mix); check the fund's Product Highlights Sheet for the actual asset allocation. EPF eligibility comes from Public Mutual's Fund Explorer. Past performance is not indicative of future performance; this is not investment advice.
